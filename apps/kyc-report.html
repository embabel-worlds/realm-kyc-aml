    How it works

    The data

    Everything comes from the kyc-aml realm in this world. The certificate you upload becomes a document in your Documents (the same store as the Documents app). Screened cases are KycCase records and their sanctions candidates are ScreeningCandidate records, both written by the screening lens. The sanctions list is the OFAC SDN list, loaded from the published sdn.xml by the realm's scripts/build-ofac-reference.py; it is only as current as that file. The header shows its publish date, read live from the SanctionsListsLoaded view.

    The questions

    • Upload — POST /api/v1/documents/upload with the file (multipart field file). Its URI is upload://<file name>.
    • Screen — lens kyc-screen-certificate, parameter document = that URI. In order: reads the document's text; an LLM maps it to a KycCase (legal name, registration number, ISO jurisdiction, incorporation date, registered office, issuer, data-quality issues — only what the document states); kyc.assessBaselineRisk scores pass 1; the name is fuzzy-matched against every OFAC record of the subject's type (primary names and aliases; token overlap or edit distance, threshold 0.72) and country, registration number and date of birth are compared; an LLM dispositions each candidate; kyc.amendCase recomputes pass 2; the case is saved. If the upload is still being ingested the app waits a few seconds and asks again.
    • Case list — view KycCases (limit 50): every screened case, newest first.
    • Report — view KycCaseReport, parameter caseId: the stored case, both risk passes, the screening result and the LLM's disposition, rendered here.
    • List status — view SanctionsListsLoaded: list, publish date and record counts.

    Labels used in the report:

    • Risk levels score LOW 20, MEDIUM 55, HIGH 90, UNKNOWN 50. Weights: ownership 30; geography, source of funds, source of wealth and AML/sanctions 15 each; document quality 10. Score = Σ(level score × weight) ÷ Σ weights. Overall risk is conservative: HIGH if any factor is HIGH, else MEDIUM if any is MEDIUM, else UNKNOWN if any is UNKNOWN, else LOW.
    • Candidate disposition: possible match until an analyst decides. Confirmed needs a near-exact name AND a matching registration number or date of birth; likely false positive needs two conflicting identifiers.
    • Sanctions factor after screening: HIGH for a confirmed match or LLM confidence ≥ 0.6, MEDIUM ≥ 0.4, LOW when nothing matched.

    When it looks wrong

    • “No screened cases yet” means the view ran and there are none. A red error means the call failed; the list may simply not have been read.
    • A subject with blank registration number or jurisdiction means the document did not state them (they are flagged as issues), not that the app lost them.
    • A CLEAR screen is only as current as the list's publish date in the header.
    • The policy numbers are demo calibration, not regulatory constants; the LLM disposition is advice, never a decision.

    What made this possible

    The page calls one lens and three views by name when you ask; there is no backend of its own. Risk scoring and case amendment run as the realm's sandboxed functions; the report is rendered here from the stored case, so reopening a case shows exactly what was decided at the time rather than re-running it. “Save as HTML” writes this report with its styles inlined; “Save as PDF” prints only the report.
